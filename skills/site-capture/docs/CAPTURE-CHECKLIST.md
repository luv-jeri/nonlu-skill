# Site Capture Completeness Checklist

This catalogue defines the evidence needed to recreate a site's look, motion, and feel across the full declared site scope and every discovered section, component, element, and state. It extends the approved `SITE-CAPTURE-PLAN.md` without changing its honesty or non-copying rules.

“Capture everything” means: exhaustively inventory the controllable states inside the declared scope, continuously record the faithful path, sample autonomous or nondeterministic behavior with declared bounds, and name every inaccessible or cap-skipped state. It never means that an unbounded site, a random simulation, or every possible frame was captured.

## Reading the catalogue

Scopes:

- **E — element:** one DOM/SVG element or one canvas surface, including associated pseudo-elements.
- **C — component:** a behavioral unit such as a card, menu, carousel, form, cursor, or scene widget.
- **S — section:** a viewport/scene-sized region and its local layer stack.
- **F — full-site:** route/state graph, global systems, shared tokens, global assets, and cross-section rhythm.
- **State dimension:** repeat the applicable E/C/S/F capture for the relevant route, viewport, scroll point, clock phase, pointer/action, UI state, and audio state.

Evidence status is field-level, not merely record-level:

- **Observed:** returned by an API, read from an accessible rule/response, or visible in a cited frame/trace.
- **Inferred:** derived from observations; store the inputs and the inference rule.
- **Unknown:** not established in covered states; record why, such as cross-origin CSS, closed shadow DOM, minified internals, canvas opacity, nondeterminism, or a cap.

Every evidence record should carry `evidenceId`, `routeId`, `viewportId`, `stateId`, `sectionId`, `componentId`, `elementId` where applicable, wall time, story/scroll progress, clock phase, pointer/action, acquisition method, status, caveat, and links to source frames or telemetry. Negative claims must be scoped: “no hover observed in the five probed positions at S03” is valid; “the site has no hover” is not.

| Authored/computed token | Resolved representation |
|---|---|
| `linear` | `cubic-bezier(0, 0, 1, 1)` plus semantic type `linear` |
| `ease` | `cubic-bezier(0.25, 0.1, 0.25, 1)` |
| `ease-in` | `cubic-bezier(0.42, 0, 1, 1)` |
| `ease-out` | `cubic-bezier(0, 0, 0.58, 1)` |
| `ease-in-out` | `cubic-bezier(0.42, 0, 0.58, 1)` |
| `step-start` | `steps(1, jump-start)` |
| `step-end` | `steps(1, jump-end)` |
| `cubic-bezier(...)`, `steps(...)`, `linear(...)` | Parsed and normalized function; preserve original string |

## Mechanical “done” gate

A capture may proceed to narrative synthesis only when:

1. The declared route/section/component boundary and every tested state dimension are recorded.
2. Every discovered item has a terminal status: captured, sampled, no behavior observed in named states, inaccessible, ambiguous, detached, or skipped by a named cap.
3. Scroll-state computed styles were read at trigger/visibility states, not only at the top of the page.
4. Every thorough scroll stop contains a settled observation, a stationary dwell series, a cursor probe record, and a post-probe deep style/animation snapshot—or an explicit reason one was skipped.
5. Transition and animation records include speed, delay, direction/fill/iteration where applicable, and both authored/computed easing plus a resolved mathematical form when that conversion is valid.
6. Authored colors, live computed colors, asset-native colors, and perceived screenshot colors remain separate datasets.
7. WebGL/canvas evidence states what is knowable and does not invent DOM elements, a scene graph, materials, camera parameters, or causality.
8. Coverage is a vector, every material claim is Observed/Inferred/Unknown, source assets are marked evidence-only, secrets are absent, and no run-owned process survives.
